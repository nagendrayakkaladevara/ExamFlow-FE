# Circulars & polls

Circulars are **announcements**. Polls collect **one vote per person** with configurable result visibility.

---

## Circulars

### Readers (all roles)

1. Open **Circulars** in the sidebar.
2. Browse the list; open an item for full content and any cover image.
3. Use share/copy link controls when shown if your staff shared a deep link.

You only see circulars targeted to you (or to a class you belong to, or a broadcast that includes your role).

### Authors (Admin & Lecturer)

1. **Circulars → New**.
2. Write title and body; optional cover image.
3. Choose **audience**:
   - Admin may target broad groups (all lecturers / all students) or specific users/classes.
   - Lecturers target **users or classes they teach** only.
4. Publish now or schedule a publish time.
5. Edit later from the circular detail/edit screens when needed.

Keep titles scannable; put critical dates in the first lines.

---

## Polls

### Voters (eligible audience)

1. Open **Polls**.
2. Enter an active poll.
3. Choose one option and submit.
4. You generally vote **once** — duplicate votes are rejected.

List labels may show whether a poll is active, expired, or already participated.

### Results visibility

| Setting | Who sees results |
| --- | --- |
| After vote | After you cast your vote |
| After expiry | After the poll end time |
| Never | Hidden from typical voters (staff/admin visibility per product rules) |

If results are hidden, the UI will not show breakdowns yet.

### Authors (Admin & Lecturer)

1. **Polls → New**.
2. Add a clear question and **at least two** options.
3. Set publish and expiry times (expiry must be after publish).
4. Choose result visibility.
5. Select audience (same scoping rules as circulars: lecturers are limited to their classes/users).

Expired polls stop accepting votes; actions that would change an expired poll may be disabled.

---

## Etiquette

- Prefer class-scoped messages over institution-wide blasts when only one cohort is affected.
- Do not put grades or personal data in circulars.
- Close polls when the decision window ends so results stay meaningful.

# FAQ

## Access & login

**I forgot my password.**  
Ask an administrator to reset it, then sign in and change it under Account → Change password.

**I am locked out after wrong passwords.**  
Wait for the lockout window to expire, then try again. If you still cannot enter, contact an administrator.

**I land on a page not found after opening a link.**  
The link may be for another role, or the item was removed. Sign in with the correct account or ask staff for an updated link.

**Can I have two roles?**  
Not on one login. Ask your administrator if you need a second account for a second role (institution policy varies).

---

## Assignments (students)

**The Start button is disabled.**  
The assignment may not have opened yet, may have closed, or you may already have a finished attempt.

**My answers are not saving.**  
Check your network indicator. Reconnect and wait for the saved confirmation before moving on.

**I ran out of time.**  
The system may auto-submit. Check Assignments for status and results policy.

**I cannot see my score.**  
Your lecturer may have set results to appear after the end time or at a scheduled release. Open the assignment later or ask them when results unlock.

---

## Assignments (lecturers)

**Students say they cannot start.**  
Verify publish state, start/end times, class enrollment, and that students use Student accounts.

**Results look empty.**  
Confirm submissions exist and you are viewing the correct assignment results page. Refresh the roster view.

---

## Circulars & polls

**I do not see a circular.**  
It may target another audience, be scheduled for later, or you may not be in the class.

**I cannot vote again.**  
Only one vote is allowed. If the poll expired, voting is closed.

**I cannot create a poll for everyone.**  
Lecturers can only target their users/classes. Ask an admin for institution-wide polls.

---

## Technical

**Which browsers work?**  
Use a current desktop or mobile browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari). Avoid very old browsers.

**Is there a mobile app?**  
ExamFlow is a web app. Mobile browsers are supported with a responsive layout; exam mode remains usable on phones with large tap targets.

**Something looks wrong visually.**  
Hard-refresh, try another browser, or reset appearance under Settings. Report persistent issues to your administrator with the page name and time.

---

## Still stuck?

Contact your lecturer for class/assignment issues, or your administrator for account and access issues. Include:

- Your role
- What you tried
- Approximate time

Never include your password in email or chat.

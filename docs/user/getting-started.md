# Getting started

## Sign in

1. Open the ExamFlow site provided by your institution.
2. Go to **Login** if you are not redirected automatically.
3. Enter your **email** and **password**.
4. You land on your role home (Admin, Lecturer, or Student dashboard).

### If login fails

- Check caps lock and email spelling.
- After several wrong attempts, the account may lock temporarily for security. Wait and try again, or contact an administrator.
- Use only the official ExamFlow URL from your institution.

---

## Find your way around

- **Sidebar** — primary sections for your role
- **Top bar** — account menu (settings, password, sign out)
- **Dashboard** — snapshot of what needs attention (assignments, activity, metrics)

On phones, open the menu from the header to reach the same sidebar links.

---

## Stay signed in safely

- ExamFlow keeps you signed in using a secure browser session.
- Always use **Sign out** on shared computers.
- Change your password if you suspect someone else knows it ([Account & settings](./settings-and-account.md)).

---

## During exams (students)

- Use a stable internet connection.
- Do not close the tab casually — the app warns you if an attempt is in progress.
- Watch the timer; unanswered questions can still be submitted, but confirm when asked.
- Answers are saved automatically as you work when the connection is healthy.

---

## Get help

1. Check the [FAQ](./faq.md).
2. Contact your lecturer (course issues) or administrator (account/access issues).
3. Include what you were doing and the approximate time — never send your password.

# Overview

## What ExamFlow does

ExamFlow helps colleges run **assessments** end to end:

- Administrators maintain users and classes and monitor institution-level activity
- Lecturers maintain a question bank, publish timed assignments, and review results
- Students take assignments in a focused exam view, with answers saved automatically where possible

You can also receive **circulars** (announcements) and participate in **polls**.

## What it is not

ExamFlow is not a full course LMS. It does not replace lecture content libraries, discussion forums, or attendance systems. It is focused on assessment, communication around assessment, and analytics.

---

## Roles

| Role | Home after login | You can… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | `/admin` | Manage users & classes, view institution analytics, publish circulars/polls broadly |
| **Lecturer** | `/lecturer` | Manage questions & assignments for your classes, view class analytics, publish scoped circulars/polls |
| **Student** | `/student` | Open assigned classes, take assignments, view results when released, vote on polls |

Your account has **one** role. If you need a different role, contact an administrator — you cannot switch roles yourself.

---

## Main areas (by role)

### Administrator

Dashboard · Users · Classes · Circulars · Polls · Analytics

### Lecturer

Dashboard · Classes · Question Bank · Assignments · Circulars · Polls · Analytics

### Student

Dashboard · Classes · Assignments · Circulars · Polls · My Performance

Everyone can open **Settings** (appearance) and **Change password** from the account menu.

---

## Design of the experience

The interface is intentionally calm and minimal so exams stay readable and low-stress. During an assignment, students use a special full-screen exam layout without the normal sidebar.

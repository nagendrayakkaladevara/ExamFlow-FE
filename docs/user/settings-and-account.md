# Account & settings

## Change password

**Path:** Account menu → Change password (or `/account/password`)

1. Enter your current password.
2. Choose a new strong password; confirm it.
3. Save.

After a successful change you must **sign in again**. If an administrator resets your password, use the temporary credentials they provide and change the password at first opportunity.

---

## Appearance

**Path:** Account menu → Settings (or `/account/settings`)

Adjust:

- **Light / dark** mode
- **Color theme** options available in your deployment (for example Default, Claude, Tangerine)

Appearance preferences are stored for convenience on your device. They do not change your exam answers or grades.

For long exams, light mode is often easier on the eyes — choose what your institution recommends.

---

## Sign out

Use **Sign out** from the account menu when you finish on a shared computer. Closing the tab alone may not fully clear a session on every device.

---

## Privacy basics

- Do not share your password.
- Do not screenshot exam content or redistribute questions.
- Treat exported analytics files as confidential.
